\bigskip
Alongside the photographs, twenty-six figures across Chapters 1--8
and 11--27 are AI-generated illustrations, produced with OpenAI image
generation from prompts written by the book's editors, and reviewed
for physical accuracy before inclusion. The full prompt for every
generated image is recorded in \texttt{images/book5/ai/PROMPTS.md} in
the repository.
