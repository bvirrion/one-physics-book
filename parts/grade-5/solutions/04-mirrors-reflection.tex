\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g5:mirrors-reflection} --- Mirrors and Reflection}

\begin{solution}{exo:g5:mirrors-reflection:1}
\omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{Light} bounces off a \omterm{def:g5:mirrors-reflection:reflection}{mirror} leaving exactly as steeply as it
arrived, on the other side --- equal angles. A ray arriving
straight-on bounces straight back along its own road.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g5:mirrors-reflection:2}
The wall's surface is a landscape of tiny bumps: each ray obeys the
bounce rule on its own little slope, so the rays scatter every
which way and the picture dissolves. The \omterm{def:g5:mirrors-reflection:reflection}{mirror}'s polish keeps all
the bounces in step --- order preserved, \omterm{prop:g5:mirrors-reflection:image}{image} preserved.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g5:mirrors-reflection:3}
Behind the glass, \qty{2}{m} deep --- so $2 + 2 = \qty{4}{m}$ from
you.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g5:mirrors-reflection:4}
The \omterm{prop:g5:mirrors-reflection:image}{image} walks toward you at the same slow step, staying exactly
as deep behind the glass as you are in front. The gap closes at
double your speed --- one step of yours plus one of the \omterm{prop:g5:mirrors-reflection:image}{image}'s.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g5:mirrors-reflection:5}
A \omterm{def:g5:mirrors-reflection:reflection}{mirror} swaps left and right, and a second \omterm{def:g5:mirrors-reflection:reflection}{mirror} swaps them
back. The word is pre-reversed so that drivers ahead, reading it
\emph{through their rear-view \omterm{def:g5:mirrors-reflection:reflection}{mirror}}, see it swapped once more ---
back to plain AMBULANCE.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g5:mirrors-reflection:6}
Twice --- once at each tilted \omterm{def:g5:mirrors-reflection:reflection}{mirror}. The top \omterm{def:g5:mirrors-reflection:reflection}{mirror} folds the
scene's \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} downward through the box; the bottom one folds it
out into the eye. Both stand at half a right angle, tilted
parallel.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g5:mirrors-reflection:7}
Tilt the \omterm{def:g5:mirrors-reflection:reflection}{mirror} until the bounced sunbeam lands on the target ---
the bounce rule makes the flash aimable; the Sun is the \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:store}{energy
store}, delivering the \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} for free. A pocket-sized flash can be
seen for kilometres.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g5:mirrors-reflection:8}
Still water is one smooth surface: bounces stay in step, and the
mountains' picture survives --- a \omterm{def:g5:mirrors-reflection:reflection}{mirror}. \omterm{def:g2:air-around-us:wind}{Wind} breaks the surface
into countless tilted patches; each patch bounces by the rule on
its own slant, the army falls out of step, and the \omterm{prop:g5:mirrors-reflection:image}{image} shatters
into dancing glitter.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g5:mirrors-reflection:9}
Each bounced ray reaches the eye from the \omterm{def:g5:mirrors-reflection:reflection}{mirror}, but the eye
prolongs it straight backward, through the glass. All the rays
bounced from your nose, prolonged backward, cross at one and the
same spot behind the wall --- so the eye builds a phantom nose
there, and phantom point by phantom point, a whole phantom you.
Agreement among rays is what makes the illusion crisp.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g5:mirrors-reflection:10}
Mount it at half a right angle to both corridors (tilted across the
corner, like the periscope's \omterm{def:g5:mirrors-reflection:reflection}{mirrors}). A ray from the aisle folds
at the \omterm{def:g5:mirrors-reflection:reflection}{mirror} and runs to the door --- and since \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light}'s roads work
both ways, a ray from the door folds along the same path back into
the aisle: each end sees the other in the same glass.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g5:mirrors-reflection:11}
One bounce reverses left and right; the periscope bounces the \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light}
twice, and the second \omterm{def:g5:mirrors-reflection:reflection}{mirror} undoes the first \omterm{def:g5:mirrors-reflection:reflection}{mirror}'s swap ---
the flip is flipped back, and the scene arrives the right way
around.
\end{solution}
