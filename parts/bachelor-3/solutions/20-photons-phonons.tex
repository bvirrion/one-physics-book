\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:photons-phonons:5}
(a) For the directed solar beam $P \approx \Phi/c =
\qty{4.5}{\micro Pa}$: eleven orders below the atmosphere ---
sails work only because space subtracts everything else. (b)
$aT^4/3 \approx \qty{1.3e13}{Pa}$: less than one part in a
thousand of the gas pressure --- the Sun is gas-supported. (c)
$P_{\text{rad}}/P_{\text{gas}} \propto T^3/n$: more massive stars
burn hotter, and the $T^4$ flood eventually rivals gravity's
grip on the gas --- luminous instability caps stars near
$\sim100\,M_\odot$. (d) $F = P/c = \qty{3.3}{\micro N}$:
tractor beams remain fiction; tweezers use field \emph{gradients}
on microscopic objects, where piconewtons rule
(\cref{pb:b3:microcanonical-ensemble:1}).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:photons-phonons:7}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:photons-phonons:9}
(a) Layer: absorbs $\sigma T_s^4$, emits $2\sigma T_a^4$ (both
faces): $T_s^4 = 2T_a^4$. Surface: $S + \sigma T_a^4 = \sigma
T_s^4$. (b) Eliminate $T_a$: $S = \tfrac12\sigma T_s^4$, i.e.\
$T_s = 2^{1/4}T_{\text{eff}}$. (c) $1.19 \times 255 =
\qty{303}{K}$ --- overshooting the real \qty{288}{K} because the
real blanket is partial and layered. (d) The vibrational quanta
of triatomic trace gases: transparent at $0.5\,\unit{\micro m}$,
opaque at $15\,\unit{\micro m}$ ---
\cref{pb:b3:harmonic-oscillator:1}'s molecule.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:photons-phonons:10}
(a) $u = (8\pi k_{\text{B}}^4T^4/h^3c^3)\int x^3/(\eu^x - 1)\dd x
= 8\pi^5k_{\text{B}}^4T^4/15h^3c^3$. (b--c) $\sigma = ac/4 =
2\pi^5k_{\text{B}}^4/15h^3c^2 = \qty{5.67e-8}{W.m^{-2}.K^{-4}}$
--- from three constants, on the nose. (d) The curve's \emph{shape}
fixes $h/k_{\text{B}}$ (through $h\nu/k_{\text{B}}T$) and its
absolute \emph{scale} fixes $k_{\text{B}}^4/h^3$: two equations,
two constants --- Planck read $h$ \emph{and} $N_{\text{A}} =
R/k_{\text{B}}$ from one fit, years before Perrin's grains.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:photons-phonons:11}
(a) $B_{12}uN_1 = B_{21}uN_2 + AN_2$. (b) Solve for $u$:
$u = (A/B_{21})/[(B_{12}/B_{21})\eu^{h\nu_0/k_{\text{B}}T} - 1]$;
matching Planck's form for all $T$ forces $B_{12} = B_{21}$ and
$A/B = 8\pi h\nu_0^3/c^3$. (c) Without $A$, no balance is possible
against \omterm{thm:b3:photons-phonons:planck}{Planck's law}: equilibrium itself demands that excited
atoms decay unprompted, at a rate tied to the stimulated one ---
Einstein predicted spontaneous emission from thermodynamics, a
decade before quantum mechanics derived it. (d) $A \propto
\nu^3$: ultraviolet states decay in nanoseconds
(\cref{exo:b3:perturbation-theory:8}), so inversions are
ruinously expensive at short wavelengths --- the X-ray laser
problem.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:photons-phonons:12}
(a) $\kappa = \tfrac13 \times \num{1.8e6} \times \num{1.3e4}
\times \num{3e-7} \approx \qty{2300}{W/(m.K)}$: five times
copper. (b) Stiff and light means fast phonons; a pure, perfect,
light lattice offers little to scatter them --- $\ell$ reaches
thousands of lattice constants. (c) Even the mass difference of
$^{13}$C atoms scatters phonons measurably: isotopic disorder is

genuine diamond feels startlingly cold --- the jeweller's touch
test is a phonon measurement.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:b3:photons-phonons:1}
\textbf{1.} $\lambda_{\max} = 2898/2.725 \approx \qty{1.06}{mm}$;
$411$ photons per cm$^3$.
\textbf{2.} $u = aT^4 = \qty{4.2e-14}{J/m^3} \approx
\qty{0.26}{eV/cm^3}$; mass-equivalent $u/c^2 \approx
\qty{4.6e-31}{kg/m^3}$: about $5\times10^{-5}$ of critical.
\textbf{3.} A thermal spectrum requires emitter and radiation to
have equilibrated --- impossible for any collection of stars,
dust or plasmas added along different sightlines; only a
universe that was \emph{once itself} a hot opaque cavity fits.
\textbf{4.} $1 + z = 3000/2.725 \approx 1100$.
\textbf{5.} Saha's equilibrium crossed to neutral atoms: free
electrons vanished, Thomson scattering ceased, and the photons
have flown untouched since.
\textbf{6.} Bright at millimetre wavelengths, at
\qty{2.725}{K}: Olbers' sky \emph{is} ablaze --- redshift moved
the blaze out of the visible.
\textbf{7.} $\num{4.1e8}/0.25 \approx \num{1.6e9}$ photons per
baryon.
\textbf{8.} The photons are the ashes of matter--antimatter
annihilation; the baryons are the one-per-billion excess that
survived: $\eta$ measures the primordial asymmetry --- still
unexplained.
\textbf{9.} $\qty{4.2e-14}{}$ against $\qty{2e-15}{J/m^3}$: the
relic light outshines, in energy density, everything the stars
have ever emitted.
\textbf{10.} Radiation dilutes as $a^{-4}$ (number $a^{-3}$,
each photon stretched $a^{-1}$), matter as $a^{-3}$: early on
radiation ruled; matter overtook at equality, letting structure
grow.
\textbf{11.} $1 + z_{\text{eq}} \approx 3400$.
\textbf{12.} $T_\nu = (4/11)^{1/3} \times 2.725 =
\qty{1.95}{K}$: a predicted cosmic neutrino background, thermal
physics' boldest unclaimed cheque.
\textbf{13.} $v = c\,\Delta T/T = \num{3e8} \times \num{1.25e-3}
\approx \qty{370}{km/s}$: the Solar System's motion through the
relic frame.
\textbf{14.} The temperature (hence density and velocity)
ripples of the universe at 380\,000 years old: a photograph of
the seeds.
\textbf{15.} Overdense patches pull harder and grow (the
gravitational instability); before equality, radiation pressure
sprang them back --- growth waited for matter to rule.
\textbf{16.} The distance sound ($c/\sqrt3$ in the
photon--baryon fluid) travelled by recombination --- the sound
horizon, an acoustic ruler buried in the sky.
\textbf{17.} The angle matches a flat universe's geometry to
about a per cent: parallel lines, cosmically, stay parallel.
\textbf{18.} Monopole: the hot beginning. Dipole: our
\qty{370}{km/s}. Ripples: the origin of structure and the
geometry of space --- each decimal place a discovery.
\textbf{19.} It was equal in every direction, day and night,
summer and winter, pigeon-free: nothing local, galactic or
instrumental is that isotropic --- only the sky itself.
\textbf{20.} A hiss on every untuned set: the Big Bang was on
television all along.
\textbf{21.} Hot cluster electrons Compton-kick CMB photons
upward in frequency: below the crossover the cluster removes
photons --- a silhouette whose depth is distance-independent,
letting surveys find clusters at any redshift.
\textbf{22.} Primordial gravitational waves would twist the
CMB's polarisation into ``B modes'': relativity's waves read in
Thomson-scattered light --- two of this book's threads knotted.
\textbf{23.} $n_\gamma c/4 \approx \qty{3e16}{m^{-2}s^{-1}}$:
about $10^{16}$ Big-Bang photons cross you per second,
unfelt.
\textbf{24.} It is a frame picked out by the \emph{contents}
(where the radiation is isotropic), not by the laws: relativity
forbids only law-preferred frames --- moving through the CMB is
detectable, moving through spacetime is not.
\textbf{25.} A \qty{2.725}{K} Planck curve on every sightline
--- $411$ photons per cubic centimetre, $10^9$ per baryon,
ripples at $10^{-5}$: statistical physics' blackbody, promoted
to the foundation document of cosmology.
\end{solution}
