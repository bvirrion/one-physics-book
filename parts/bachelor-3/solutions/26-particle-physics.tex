\begin{solution}{exo:b3:particle-physics:1}
(a) uud: $\tfrac23 + \tfrac23 - \tfrac13 = +1$; udd: $\tfrac23 -
\tfrac13 - \tfrac13 = 0$. (b) $\tfrac23 + \tfrac13 = +1$ (the
anti-down carries $+\tfrac13$). (c) $\pi^- =
\bar{\text{u}}\text{d}$; $\Delta^{++} = $ uuu ($3\times\tfrac23 =
+2$ --- the state whose statistics helped force the invention of
colour). (d) The stretched gluon field stores energy
$\propto$ length; long before a \omterm{def:b3:particle-physics:zoo}{quark} is free, the stored energy
exceeds $2m_{\text{q}}c^2$ and the string snaps into a fresh
pair: you pull out a \omterm{def:b3:particle-physics:zoo}{meson}, never a bare \omterm{def:b3:particle-physics:zoo}{quark}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:particle-physics:2}

\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:particle-physics:3}
(a) Allowed --- standard $\beta^-$ decay (the free neutron does
it, $T_{1/2} \approx \qty{10}{min}$). (b) Forbidden by energy:
the products outweigh the free proton (inside a nucleus, \omterm{prop:b3:nuclear-physics:binding}{binding
energy} can pay, and bound $\beta^+$ decay happens). (c)
Forbidden: \omterm{prop:b3:particle-physics:conservation}{baryon number} $1 \to 0$ (and \omterm{prop:b3:particle-physics:conservation}{lepton number}
$0 \to -1$). (d) Forbidden by \emph{family} \omterm{prop:b3:particle-physics:conservation}{lepton number}:
muon-ness would vanish and electron-ness appear --- never
observed, and hunted precisely for that reason.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:particle-physics:4}
(a) $6\times0.938 \approx \qty{5.6}{GeV}$. (b) Kinetic energy
$m_{\text{p}}c^2 \approx \qty{0.94}{GeV}$ per beam

\omterm{def:b3:relativistic-dynamics:four-momentum}{invariant mass}, and the antiproton arrived on schedule.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:particle-physics:5}
(a) $c\tau = \qty{660}{m}$. (b) A dilated range of
${\sim}\qty{15}{km}$ needs $\gamma \sim 23$: $E = \gamma
m_\mu c^2 \sim \qty{2.4}{GeV}$ --- exactly the cosmic scale. (c)
In its own frame the muon lives its plain \qty{2.2}{\micro s}
while the atmosphere rushes past contracted to
${\sim}\qty{650}{m}$: same survival, other ledger. (d) \omterm{prop:b3:particle-physics:conservation}{Baryon
number}: there is no \omterm{def:b3:particle-physics:zoo}{baryon} light enough, and the muon is a
\omterm{def:b3:particle-physics:zoo}{lepton}; its decay must change flavour, which only the weak force
does --- hence stately microseconds instead of strong-force
yoctoseconds.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:particle-physics:6}
(a) $\text{d} \to \text{u} + \text{e}^- + \bar\nu_{\text{e}}$:
charge $-\tfrac13 = \tfrac23 - 1 + 0$; \omterm{prop:b3:particle-physics:conservation}{baryon number}
$\tfrac13 = \tfrac13$; electron-family number $0 = 1 - 1$. (b) A
two-body decay fixes the electron's energy; the observed
\emph{continuum} meant either energy conservation failed
(Bohr entertained it) or a third, invisible body shared the
budget --- Pauli's neutrino, detected twenty-five years later.
(c) $\hbar/m_{\text{W}}c = 197/80400 \approx
\qty{2.5e-3}{fm}$ --- a thousandth of a proton. (d) At low
energies the interaction must bridge a gap its heavy mediator
makes absurdly short, so amplitudes are tiny; near
\qty{100}{GeV} the W is affordable and the ``weak'' force shows
its true electroweak strength.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:particle-physics:7}
(a) The strong force conserves strangeness, so it can only make
$+1$ and $-1$ together: a kaon with its hyperon. (b) Thirteen
orders of magnitude too slow for the strong force: the weak
force, sole breaker of flavour, signs the death certificate.
(c) $10^{13}$: one second against three hundred thousand years.
(d) Copious birth said ``strong''; reluctant death said ``not
strong'' --- only a new conserved number, made by the strong
force in pairs and broken by the weak, reconciles the two: the
bookkeeping \emph{was} the discovery.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:particle-physics:8}
(a) In the pair's rest frame the total momentum is zero: one
photon can never have zero momentum, two back-to-back can ---
conservation writes PET's geometry. (b) $E = mc^2 =
0.002\times(3\times10^8)^2 = \qty{1.8e14}{J}$: two grams of
annihilation outdo a fissioned kilogram of uranium. (c) From
cyclotron-made $\beta^+$ \omterm{def:b3:nuclear-physics:nuclide}{isotopes} --- $^{18}$F, built into
glucose: the body's own chemistry places the positron source in
the hungriest cells. (d) A fuel that costs a million times its
yield, with no natural mines: antimatter is a detector
calibration and a storyteller's engine, not an energy source.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:particle-physics:9}
(a) The Higgs potential is a double well (a Mexican hat): the
symmetric point $\phi = 0$ is a summit, and the vacuum rolled
into the rim --- the universe's ground state broke its own
symmetry, exactly like the magnet choosing north. (b) The choice
breaks only part of the symmetry: the combination that survives
is electromagnetism, so its carrier --- the photon --- keeps
zero mass, while W and Z, carriers of the broken directions,
eat the corresponding stiffness as mass. (c) The radial
oscillation of the \omterm{def:b3:phase-transitions:order}{order parameter} --- in the magnet, the
fluctuation of $|m|$ about its equilibrium value near
$T_{\text{c}}$. (d) In the first $10^{-11}$ seconds of the
universe (\cref{ch:b3:astrophysics}): above the electroweak
temperature every particle was massless and the two forces were
one.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:particle-physics:10}
(a) Each \qty{26.7}{MeV} $= \qty{4.28e-12}{J}$ delivers two
neutrinos: flux $= 2\times1360/4.28\times10^{-12} \approx
\qty{6.4e14}{m^{-2}.s^{-1}}$. (b) Through a thumbnail:
$6\times10^{10}$ per second. Lifetime passages through your
${\sim}\qty{0.03}{m^2}$, \qty{0.3}{m}-thick body over
${\sim}\qty{2.5e9}{s}$: about $5\times10^{22}$, times
$10^{-22}\,\unit{m^{-1}}\times\qty{0.3}{m}$: order one ---
a single solar neutrino will touch you in your whole life. (c)
The missing $\nu_{\text{e}}$ had oscillated into $\nu_\mu$ and
$\nu_\tau$ en route; oscillation requires mass differences, so
neutrinos have mass --- the first laboratory crack in the
massless-neutrino Standard Model. (d) The mountain filters out
everything \emph{else}: only neutrinos pass, so kilometres of
rock turn the noisiest sky into the quietest laboratory.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:particle-physics:11}
(a) $\gamma = 6.8\times10^{12}/9.38\times10^{8} \approx 7250$:
$1 - v/c \approx 1/2\gamma^2 \approx 10^{-8}$ --- three metres
per second short of light. (b) $\lambda \approx hc/E \approx
\qty{3e-5}{fm}$: thirty-thousandth of a proton radius --- \omterm{def:b3:particle-physics:zoo}{quark}
territory. (c) $13.6\,\text{TeV}/938\,\text{MeV} \approx
14500$ proton masses --- were all of it convertible. (d) At
fixed energy $\gamma_{\text{e}}/\gamma_{\text{p}} =
m_{\text{p}}/m_{\text{e}} \approx 1836$, so electron losses are
$1836^4 \sim 10^{13}$ worse: protons for brute-force rings,
electrons (clean, point-like) for precision at lower energy.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:b3:particle-physics:12}
(a) $3\times10^{20}\times1.6\times10^{-19} \approx \qty{48}{J}$:
a hard-served tennis ball --- in one proton. (b) $\gamma \approx
3\times10^{11}$; the Galaxy's $10^{5}$ light-years contract to
${\sim}10$ light-\emph{seconds}: it crosses the Milky Way, by
its own clock, in seconds. (c) In the proton's frame the CMB's
meek microwaves arrive blueshifted into $\gamma$-rays energetic
enough to knock pions off it: above the threshold the universe
itself is an absorber, and such protons must be local. (d) One
such particle per square kilometre per century, of unknown
species, aimed by nobody: the LHC's $10^{9}$ collisions per
second, of known beams at known energy, buy statistics and
control that no cosmic gift can match.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:b3:particle-physics:1}
\textbf{1.} A primary proton (often \unit{GeV}--\unit{TeV})
strikes a nucleus high up (strong force), spraying pions; the
charged pions decay by the weak force to muons and neutrinos;
the muons, ionising only gently (electromagnetic), race for the
ground.
\textbf{2.} $c\tau_\pi \approx \qty{8}{m}$: even large $\gamma$
buys pions only hundreds of metres, and they also die by \omterm{rem:b3:particle-physics:forces}{strong
interactions} in air; the muon, feeling no strong force and
living $85\times$ longer, is the one that travels.
\textbf{3.} $400\,\unit{cm^2}\times1\,\text{min}^{-1} = 400$
per minute --- about 7 per second.
\textbf{4.} Noise flashes and local radioactivity fire one
paddle at random; firing \emph{both} within \qty{100}{ns}
demands a single particle traversing the pair --- geometry as a
filter.
\textbf{5.} $R_{\text{acc}} = R_1R_2\Delta t = 100\times100
\times10^{-7} = \qty{e-3}{s^{-1}}$ --- one false count per
quarter hour against a hundred muons a minute: negligible.
\textbf{6.} Only muons within the cone that threads both
paddles (near-vertical, within ${\sim}30$--$40^\circ$) are
accepted: the pair measures a direction, not just a rate ---
that is what makes it a \emph{telescope}.
\textbf{7.} Slanted muons cross more atmosphere: more energy
loss and more \omterm{prop:b3:relativistic-kinematics:dilation}{proper time} in flight, so fewer survive ---
roughly the $\cos^2\theta$ law your tilted runs trace.
\textbf{8.} $c\tau = \qty{660}{m}$: a classical muon from
\qty{15}{km} survives with probability
$\eu^{-15000/660} \approx 10^{-10}$ --- the attic would count
one muon a decade.
\textbf{9.} $\gamma = 4000/106 \approx 38$.
\textbf{10.} Dilated lifetime $\gamma\tau \approx
\qty{84}{\micro s}$: range ${\sim}\qty{25}{km}$ --- the attic
rate is exactly what relativity predicts.
\textbf{11.} In the muon's frame the lifetime is the plain
\qty{2.2}{\micro s} but the atmosphere is contracted to
$15000/38 \approx \qty{400}{m}$: crossed with time to spare.
\textbf{12.} $t_{\text{proper}} \approx 15000/(38\times3\times
10^8) = \qty{1.3}{\micro s}$: survival $\eu^{-1.3/2.2} \approx
0.55$ --- half arrive, and your counter is their census.
\textbf{13.} The classic mountain-versus-sea-level muon
experiments (Rossi--Hall, and Frisch--Smith on film): classical
decay predicts essentially zero counts; your hundreds per
minute are special relativity, verified above the insulation.
\textbf{14.} A charged \omterm{def:b3:particle-physics:zoo}{lepton} of the second generation:
charge $-e$, spin $\tfrac12$, mass \qty{106}{MeV} --- the
electron's heavy twin.
\textbf{15.} Charge: $-1 = -1 + 0 + 0$; electron-family:
$0 = 1 + (-1) + 0$; muon-family: $1 = 0 + 0 + 1$. All books
balance.
\textbf{16.} Three bodies share the budget in all proportions,
so the electron's spectrum is continuous; it ends when the two
neutrinos recoil together against the electron: about
$m_\mu c^2/2 \approx \qty{53}{MeV}$.
\textbf{17.} It would break lepton-family number --- the one
book the Standard Model balances without knowing why. Finding
it would be the charged-sector cousin of neutrino oscillations:
guaranteed new physics, which is why experiments chase it to
one part in $10^{13}$.
\textbf{18.} The muon's entry makes one flash; microseconds
later its decay electron makes a second in the same paddle. The
histogram of delays is an exponential whose slope \emph{is}
$\tau = \qty{2.2}{\micro s}$: a lifetime measured on a shelf.
\textbf{19.} All Bohr formulas scale with mass: the muonic
hydrogen orbit shrinks by $207$ to ${\sim}\qty{250}{fm}$ ---
so deep inside the electron cloud that the muon feels the
proton's size: muonic atoms are proton rulers.
\textbf{20.} $10^{15}/10^{6} = \qty{e9}{eV}$: about a GeV per
particle --- your attic muons are average shower citizens.
\textbf{21.} Relativistic muons outrun light in water and glow
Cherenkov blue into the tank's photomultipliers. A
$10^{20}\,\unit{eV}$ shower's footprint spans many square
kilometres, so sparse tanks sample it like raindrops in
buckets.
\textbf{22.} A particle deposits energy in a medium that
converts it to light, a photomultiplier converts the light to
electrons, and electronics counts them: every calorimeter from
attic to collider is this sentence.
\textbf{23.} The curl's radius gives momentum, its sense gives
the charge's sign; the photon, the neutron and the neutrino ---
neutral --- leave no track, only their debts in the
bookkeeping.
\textbf{24.} Nature accelerates single protons ten million
times beyond the LHC --- humbling --- but at one per square
kilometre per century: our machines trade peak energy for
$10^{9}$ controlled collisions a second.
\textbf{25.} Born of a proton ten kilometres up; delivered
alive by \omterm{prop:b3:relativistic-kinematics:dilation}{time dilation} ($\gamma \approx 38$, half surviving);
identified by two flashes a hundred nanoseconds apart; and
explained, entirely, by a table with twelve seats and four
forces.
\end{solution}
