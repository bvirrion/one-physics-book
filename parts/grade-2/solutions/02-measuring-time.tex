\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g2:measuring-time} --- Measuring Time}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:measuring-time:1}
Wake up, breakfast, afternoon playtime, dinner, bed.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:measuring-time:2}
Moment; \omterm{def:g2:measuring-time:duration}{duration}; moment; \omterm{def:g2:measuring-time:duration}{duration}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:measuring-time:3}
The blue candle lasted longer. The candles started at the same
moment, and the one still burning when the other finished is the
longer-lasting one --- exactly the fair-race rule.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:measuring-time:4}
Every time it is turned, the sand takes the \emph{same} \omterm{def:g2:measuring-time:duration}{duration} to
pour through the narrow waist. A referee that never changes is fair
to everyone.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:measuring-time:5}
Our feeling of time is a bad clock: boring waits crawl and fun flies.
The watch counts steadily whatever Ben feels, so the wait really was
shorter than the cartoon --- it only \emph{felt} longer.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:measuring-time:6}
Answers vary; typically a few seconds for the name and around
$20$ seconds for the shoes. The counting must stay calm and steady.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:measuring-time:7}
A sneeze: seconds. A night of sleep: \omterm{ex:g2:measuring-time:clock}{hours}. Tying a shoelace:
seconds. A school morning: \omterm{ex:g2:measuring-time:clock}{hours}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:measuring-time:8}
At five o'clock the short hand points at the $5$ (long hand straight
up); at half past five the short hand points \emph{halfway between}
the $5$ and the $6$ (long hand straight down).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:measuring-time:9}
Race each against the same sand timer: at bath time, start the timer
and count how many full turns of the timer the bath lasts; tomorrow,
do the same for the shower. Write down both counts (say, ``bath: $4$
turns, shower: $2$ turns''): more turns means longer, and anyone with
the same timer can check.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g2:measuring-time:10}
Tom is closer. Counting seconds is honest only at a calm, steady
pace --- about one calm heartbeat per number, or ``one little
crocodile'' per count. Racing through the numbers makes the seconds
too short.
\end{solution}
