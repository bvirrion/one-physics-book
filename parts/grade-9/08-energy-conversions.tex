\chapter{Forms of Energy and Conversions}\label{ch:g9:energy-conversions}

\omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{Energy} has run through this book like a river under a city ---
glimpsed at every corner, never yet mapped whole. The time has
come. Every form gets its name, one of them gets this year's
second great formula, and over them all presides the mightiest
bookkeeping law in science: the total never changes. Ever.

\section{The forms, assembled}

\begin{definition}[The forms of energy]\label{def:g9:energy-conversions:forms}
The forms of \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{energy}, formally presented:
\emph{\omterm{prop:g9:kinetic-energy-safety:formula}{kinetic energy}} --- motion's share, $\frac12 m v^2$;
\emph{potential energy}\index{potential energy} --- stored by
position or arrangement (the lifted, the stretched, the
\omterm{prop:g7:states-of-matter:squeeze}{compressed}); \emph{thermal \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{energy}} --- the disordered
molecular jiggling that \omterm{def:g3:temperature-thermometers:temperature}{temperature} \omterm{def:g6:measurement-in-science:measuring}{measures};
\emph{chemical \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{energy}} --- stored in matter's arrangements:
food, fuels, batteries; \emph{electrical \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{energy}} --- the
marching current's deliverable; \emph{radiant \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{energy}} ---
carried by \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} itself, sunshine's shipping form; and
\emph{nuclear \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{energy}} --- locked in the atom's core, the
deepest store humans have opened.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Gravitational potential energy]\label{prop:g9:energy-conversions:ep}
Lifting a \omterm{def:g3:measuring-mass:mass}{mass} $m$ (in \unit{kg}) through a height $h$ (in
\unit{m}) against gravity's $g$ stores in it the \omterm{def:g9:energy-conversions:forms}{potential
energy}
\[
E_p = m \times g \times h
\]
\omterm{def:g9:kinetic-energy-safety:joule}{joules} --- recoverable in full on the way back down. The
lifted axe, the drawn-back swing, the mountain lake: all hold
$m g h$ on account. (This is the borrowed formula of the
road-safety posters --- now officially yours.)
\end{proposition}

\begin{example}[The great trade: $E_p$ against $E_k$]\label{ex:g9:energy-conversions:trade}
Drop a ball from height $h$: as it falls, its account
transfers, \omterm{def:g9:kinetic-energy-safety:joule}{joule} by \omterm{def:g9:kinetic-energy-safety:joule}{joule}, from potential to kinetic. At the
ground, the whole $m g h$ has become $\frac12 m v^2$ --- set
them equal and the \omterm{def:g3:measuring-mass:mass}{mass} cancels:
\[
v = \sqrt{2 g h},
\]
this year's square root earning its keep. From \qty{20}{m}:
$v = \sqrt{2 \times 9.8 \times 20} \approx \qty{20}{m/s}$ ---
seventy kilometres per hour, \omterm{def:g3:measuring-mass:mass}{mass} be what it may. The
pendulum plays the trade both ways forever (minus a whisper
to the \omterm{def:g2:air-around-us:air}{air}); the skateboarder in the half-pipe likewise:
height to \omterm{def:g7:motion-average-speed:formula}{speed} to height, the two formulas passing one sum
back and forth.
\end{example}

\section{The great law}

\begin{proposition}[Conservation of energy]\label{prop:g9:energy-conversions:conservation}
In every process ever examined --- collisions and chemistry,
machines and storms, \omterm{def:g4:solar-system:star}{stars} and cells --- \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{energy} changes form
and owner, but the \emph{total} is exactly conserved: none
created, none destroyed, every \omterm{def:g9:kinetic-energy-safety:joule}{joule} accounted for. This is
the deepest bookkeeping law in physics: it retired the
forever-machines of the childhood chapters, it audits every
chain of this book, and no experiment in three centuries has
caught it in an error. (Why nature keeps this law so
faithfully is a profound question with a beautiful answer ---
one of the jewels of the university years.)
\end{proposition}

\begin{method}[Auditing any process]\label{met:g9:energy-conversions:audit}
The physicist's universal bookkeeping:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item fence off the system --- what is inside the audit;
  \item list the \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{energy} accounts before: each form, each
        store;
  \item list them after --- including, always, the thermal
        small change: friction's warmth, \omterm{def:g3:sound-around-us:sound}{sound}'s whisper;
  \item balance the books: the totals must match. A deficit
        means a form went uncounted --- look again for the
        leak; it is usually warm.
\end{enumerate}
\end{method}

\begin{example}[Audits, worked]\label{ex:g9:energy-conversions:audits}
The braking car: kinetic account emptied, thermal account
at the discs credited in full --- retirement, not
destruction. The bouncing ball that dies away: each bounce
skims kinetic into warmth of ball and floor; the childhood
answer, now auditable. Your breakfast cycling uphill:
chemical store down, potential account up ($m g h$ of you
and bicycle), thermal share radiated --- muscles run warm.
Every mystery of the early \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{energy} chapters closes under the
same three columns.
\end{example}

\section{Efficiency}

\begin{definition}[Efficiency]\label{def:g9:energy-conversions:efficiency}
The \emph{efficiency}\index{efficiency} of a converter is the
fraction of its input \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{energy} delivered in the wanted form:
\[
\text{efficiency} =
\frac{\text{useful energy out}}{\text{total energy in}},
\]
a number between $0$ and $1$ (or its percentage). The
remainder is not destroyed --- conservation forbids --- but
escapes in unwanted forms, nearly always warmth.
\end{definition}

\begin{example}[Report cards]\label{ex:g9:energy-conversions:reportcards}
The glowing ancestor \omterm{def:g3:first-electric-circuit:bulb}{bulb}: five percent \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light}, ninety-five
warmth --- a heater with a hobby. Its modern successor:
thirty-plus percent, the rest still warmth. A car engine:
about a third useful motion, two thirds out the radiator and
exhaust. An electric \omterm{ex:g6:circuits-and-safety:motor}{motor}: above ninety. The great
\omterm{def:g9:alternator:alternator}{alternators}: near ninety-nine --- civilization's finest
converters. No honest converter reaches one hundred: some
leak to warmth is nature's universal commission.
\end{example}

\begin{remark}[What ``saving energy'' really means]\label{rem:g9:energy-conversions:saving}
If conservation guards every \omterm{def:g9:kinetic-energy-safety:joule}{joule}, why must anyone ``save
\omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{energy}''? Because the law preserves \emph{amount}, not
\emph{usefulness}. Concentrated stores --- fuel, charge,
height --- can be spent into warmth spread thinly through the
world, and spread warmth, though every \omterm{def:g9:kinetic-energy-safety:joule}{joule} of it survives,
can no longer drive kettles or trains: the river ran
downhill. Civilization's \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{energy} problem is not a shortage of
\omterm{def:g9:kinetic-energy-safety:joule}{joules} --- the Sun showers us with thousands of times our
needs --- but the husbanding of \emph{useful} ones. That
idea, made precise, is one of the most celebrated stories in
physics; it waits in the university years under its famous
name.
\end{remark}

\section{Problem: The Theme Park Commission}

\begin{problem}[{Weekend problem --- the theme park hires a
physics consultant; coasters, towers and the ledger that
cannot lie}]\label{pb:g9:energy-conversions:1}
The park's new attractions must pass your \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{energy} audit
before opening day. Take $g = \qty{9.8}{N/kg}$; ignore
friction until it refuses to be ignored.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Part I --- The Great Drop.}
A \qty{2000}{kg} coaster train is winched to a \qty{45}{m}
summit and released from rest.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The train's $E_p$ at the summit?
  \item Its ideal \omterm{def:g7:motion-average-speed:formula}{speed} at the bottom of the drop, by the
        mass-canceling formula --- in \unit{m/s} and
        \unit{km/h}?
  \item The designers add a second hill of \qty{50}{m}
        just after the drop. Veto it with the ledger ---
        and state the tallest second hill the ideal train
        could crest.
  \item The measured bottom \omterm{def:g7:motion-average-speed:formula}{speed} is \qty{28}{m/s}, not
        the ideal. Compute both kinetic energies and
        charge the difference to the proper account.
\end{enumerate}

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Part II --- The winch and the
bill.}
\begin{enumerate}[resume]
  \item The winch lifts the train to its summit in
        \qty{60}{s}. At perfect \omterm{def:g9:energy-conversions:efficiency}{efficiency}, its \omterm{def:g9:electric-power-energy:power}{power}?
  \item The real winch draws \qty{20}{kW}. Its
        \omterm{def:g9:energy-conversions:efficiency}{efficiency}?
  \item Each ride runs every four minutes, ten \omterm{ex:g2:measuring-time:clock}{hours} a
        day. The winch's daily \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{energy} in \omterm{def:g9:electric-power-energy:kwh}{kilowatt-hours}
        (real winch, count the lifts), and its cost at
        $0.25$ per \omterm{def:g6:measurement-in-science:measuring}{unit}?
  \item A designer proposes recovering the train's arrival
        \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{energy} with a generator-brake feeding the grid ---
        ``the ride pays for itself!'' Audit the dream:
        what fraction could realistically return, and
        which law forbids the full circle?
\end{enumerate}

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Part III --- The freefall tower
and the report.}
The \qty{60}{m} tower drops a \qty{500}{kg} gondola into
magnetic brakes at \qty{10}{m}.
\begin{enumerate}[resume]
  \item \omterm{def:g7:motion-average-speed:formula}{Speed} entering the brake zone (a \qty{50}{m} ideal
        fall)?
  \item \omterm{def:g5:everyday-energy:energy}{Energy} the brakes must retire, and where induction
        sends it. (The brakes are the \omterm{def:g9:alternator:alternator}{alternator} chapter's
        cousins --- eddies of induced current heating
        metal.)
  \item Riders scream that they were ``weightless'' during
        the fall. From the falling-companions idea of the
        \omterm{def:g9:gravitation:gravitation}{gravitation} chapter, adjudicate the claim in one
        sentence.
  \item Sign the commission's report: three sentences ---
        the trade the coaster lives by, the law every
        attraction obeyed, and the account that quietly
        taxed them all.
\end{enumerate}
\end{problem}
