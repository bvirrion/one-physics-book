\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g1:magnets} --- Magnets}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:magnets:1}
Grabbed: the nail, the paperclip, the steel spoon. Ignored: the cork,
the plastic duck, the sheet of paper.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:magnets:2}
Iron (and steel, which is mostly iron). Ignored anyway: a coin and
kitchen foil --- metals, but not iron.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:magnets:3}
The sheet of paper (the drawing) is between them. The \omterm{def:g1:magnets:magnet}{magnet}'s pull
goes right through the paper to the iron door --- it works through
things.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:magnets:4}
Slide the \omterm{def:g1:magnets:magnet}{magnet} against the \emph{outside} of the glass, next to the
clip, and walk it slowly up the side: the clip follows inside, up to
the rim, pulled through the glass and the water.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:magnets:5}
Far from the \omterm{def:g1:magnets:magnet}{magnet} the pull is too weak to move the clip; it grows
strong only close up. The \omterm{def:g1:magnets:magnet}{magnet}'s reach fades quickly with distance.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:magnets:6}
Turning one of the two \omterm{def:g1:magnets:magnet}{magnets} around, so that the other end comes
first. Then they push apart instead of snapping together.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:magnets:7}
At the ends --- clips mostly ignore the middle. A \omterm{def:g1:magnets:magnet}{magnet} is strongest
at its two ends.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:magnets:8}
Alike: both can pull a thing toward you. Different: your hand must
touch the toy, while the \omterm{def:g1:magnets:magnet}{magnet} pulls the clip across empty air,
through wood and through water, touching nothing.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g1:magnets:9}
Hold the \omterm{def:g1:magnets:magnet}{magnet} near each object in turn. The nail jumps to it; the
coin and the foil do not move, though both are metal. So Tom's
sentence is wrong as stated; corrected: ``\omterm{def:g1:magnets:magnet}{Magnets} \omterm{def:g1:magnets:magnet}{attract} things made
of iron (or steel), not every metal.''
\end{solution}
