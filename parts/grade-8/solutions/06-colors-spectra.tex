\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g8:colors-spectra} --- Colors and Spectra of Light}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:colors-spectra:1}
The continuous band of colors hiding in white \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light}, unpacked;
\omterm{prop:g8:colors-spectra:mixture}{dispersion} is the unpacking --- each color bent its own amount.
Red bends least, violet most.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:colors-spectra:2}
The rainbow forms in the half of the sky opposite the Sun ---
sunlight enters the raindrops from behind the hunter. Every
raindrop plays the prism (with a mirror-bounce inside).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:colors-spectra:3}
Unpacking: the prism fans white \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} into the \omterm{prop:g8:colors-spectra:mixture}{spectrum}.
Packing: the spinning rainbow disc blurs to whitish --- and the
second prism folds a \omterm{prop:g8:colors-spectra:mixture}{spectrum} back into one white \omterm{def:g6:light-propagation:ray}{beam}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:colors-spectra:4}
Grass absorbs most of the blend and returns chiefly green. The
page returns nearly everything --- white; the blackboard
returns nearly nothing, absorbing the lot --- black.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:colors-spectra:5}
White \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light}: the scarf returns its yellow --- yellow scarf.
Blue \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light}: the offer (blue) meets the answer (yellow) in an
empty intersection --- the scarf shows black.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:colors-spectra:6}
The apple can return only red, and the blue flood brings none:
black. The handkerchief returns whatever arrives --- offered
blue, it returns blue: a blue handkerchief.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:colors-spectra:7}
Red, green, blue. Red $+$ green: yellow. Green $+$ blue: cyan.
Blue $+$ red: magenta. All three: white.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:colors-spectra:8}
Only minute red, green and blue strips, all lit --- no white
anywhere. The blending happens in your eye, too far away to
separate the strips.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:colors-spectra:9}
\omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{Lights} add what they bring, and the full set of colors
together is white --- addition completes the blend. Paints
absorb, each subtracting its share from the \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light}, and the
full set of subtractions leaves almost nothing --- darkness by
committee.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:colors-spectra:10}
The \omterm{def:g2:air-around-us:air}{air} steals blue preferentially; the whole sky receives the
stolen blue and forwards it downward as daytime's blue dome;
the red-orange remainder survives the grazing \omterm{def:g1:day-and-night:daynight}{sunset} road. The
eclipsed \omterm{def:g2:moon-in-the-sky:moon}{Moon} is lit only by \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} that has grazed the Earth's
rim --- sunset-filtered all around the \omterm{def:g4:solar-system:planet}{planet} --- so it glows
with the same red survivor.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:colors-spectra:11}
The warm reddish lamps offered little green; the jacket's true
answer --- mostly green with some red making a dull blend ---
could only show its red-friendly part, which read as warm
brown. Daylight's full offer let the green answer speak:
deception by a stingy \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light}, convicted by the intersection
rule.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:colors-spectra:12}
Letters blue, ground black. Under white houselights the blue
letters return their blue while the black ground returns
nothing: bright blue lettering on black --- readable. Under the
deep red wash, the blue letters are offered nothing they can
return and the black ground returns nothing as always: uniform
black, banner vanished. White lettering would betray the
finale: the universal returner answers even a red wash with
bright red letters, legible against the black ground.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g8:colors-spectra:1}
\textbf{1.} White --- the three primaries at full strength add
to the complete blend.
\textbf{2.} Yellow.
\textbf{3.} White shirt: returns the offer --- yellow. Blue
sash: offered no blue --- black. Yellow gown: yellow's answer
matches the yellow offer --- brilliant yellow.
\textbf{4.} Blue backdrop. White cloth returns whatever the
designer sends: one backdrop, every color on demand --- the
parallel of the white screen at the cinema.
\textbf{5.} A green coat: under the red wash its green answer
meets no green --- black villain; under white \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} the full
offer lets the green speak --- rich green.
\textbf{6.} A white robe --- the universal returner: red under
the red flood, green under green, blue under blue, white under
all three; always the brightest thing on stage, always
``white-looking'' relative to the scene.
\textbf{7.} Any lighting \emph{without} red: the blue flood,
the green flood, or both together --- offered no red, the
scarf returns nothing and \omterm{def:g2:ice-water-steam:meltfreeze}{melts} into the black curtain. The
instant the red flood joins, the offer contains exactly what
the scarf answers: it blazes red, and the trick dies.
\textbf{8.} Gold-as-warm-yellow answers red and green
generously, blue barely. The blue-heavy preset offered
little it could return: muddy, dim lettering. Noon's full
blend let the yellow blaze.
\textbf{9.} Red stripe: brilliant red. Orange and yellow:
dimmed reddish versions of themselves (their answers include
some red). Green: black. Blue and violet: black (violet
barely ember-dark). The arch collapses to a red-and-black
ruin.
\textbf{10.} Red and blue stripes revive; violet glows
faintly (it answers blue and some red); orange and yellow
show only their weak red shares; the green stripe stays
black --- magenta \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} contains no green.
\textbf{11.} Only white \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} --- the full blend --- shows
all six stripes: each stripe returns its own color, and only
an offer containing all six can be answered by all six. A
green flood, however bright, \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{lights} one stripe and murders
five.
\textbf{12.} For example: ``Mixed \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{lights} \emph{add}: red,
green and blue build every color and, together, white.
And every object \emph{subtracts}: it returns only its own
share of what the \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} brought --- offer it nothing it can
return, and it dresses in black.''
\end{solution}
