\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g8:lenses-images} --- Lenses and Images}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:lenses-images:1}
A middle bulge: converging --- it bends a parallel \omterm{def:g6:light-propagation:ray}{beam}'s rays
toward each other, gathering them at the focus. A middle
hollow: diverging --- it fans the \omterm{def:g6:light-propagation:ray}{beam} apart.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:lenses-images:2}
The \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:focus}{focal point} is where a parallel \omterm{def:g6:light-propagation:ray}{beam} gathers after the
\omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens}; the \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:focus}{focal length} $f$ is the lens-to-focus distance. A
short $f$ means strong curvature and hard bending.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:lenses-images:3}
Throw the distant scene's \omterm{prop:g5:mirrors-reflection:image}{image} onto a wall, sharpen by moving
the \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens}, and \omterm{def:g6:measurement-in-science:measuring}{measure} lens-to-wall: that distance is $f$. The
scene must be distant so its rays arrive practically parallel
--- and parallel rays, by definition of the focus, gather at
exactly $f$.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:lenses-images:4}
Never focus sunlight near anything flammable --- the focal dot
chars and ignites in seconds. Never look at the Sun through
any \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens} --- eye plus \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens} focus the burn onto the retina,
instantly and painlessly.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:lenses-images:5}
A picture genuinely assembled by gathered rays, catchable on a
screen. Signatures: inverted, and bright --- the \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens} harvests
a whole lensful of \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} per point where the pinhole passed
one thread.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:lenses-images:6}
\omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{Lens}: the glass objective --- the eye's soft \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens} behind the
pupil. Screen: the sensor --- the retina. Focusing: the camera
slides its \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens}; the eye reshapes its \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens}, fatter for near,
thinner for far.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:lenses-images:7}
Short sight focuses too strongly --- \omterm{prop:g5:mirrors-reflection:image}{images} assemble before
the retina: a \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{diverging lens} un-bends just enough. The aging
long-sighted eye focuses too weakly for near work: a
\omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{converging lens} lends the missing strength.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:lenses-images:8}
The stamp must sit closer to the \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens} than its \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:focus}{focal length}.
The eye then enjoys an upright, enlarged phantom --- rays bent
as if from a grander stamp behind the glass; nothing lands on
any screen.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:lenses-images:9}
As the object nears, its \omterm{prop:g5:mirrors-reflection:image}{image} retreats --- so the camera
moves its \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens} \emph{away} from the sensor to chase the
retreating \omterm{prop:g5:mirrors-reflection:image}{image}. The eye instead tugs its \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens} fatter,
bending harder to pull the \omterm{prop:g5:mirrors-reflection:image}{image} back onto the fixed retina.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:lenses-images:10}
At the \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:focus}{focal length}: only there does the \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens} crowd the Sun's
practically-parallel rays into their smallest, fiercest dot.
Nearer or farther, the \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} spreads over a patch too gentle
to ignite --- the fire waits precisely at $f$.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:lenses-images:11}
Reading glasses are converging: they gather sunlight to a
burning focus and magnify close print --- both converging-only
talents. The short-sighted grandson's \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{diverging lenses} spread
every \omterm{def:g6:light-propagation:ray}{beam}: no focus, no burning dot, and print viewed through
them shrinks instead of growing.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:lenses-images:12}
Telescope, stage one: the great \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens} receives a \omterm{def:g4:solar-system:star}{star}'s
practically-parallel rays and gathers them into a small \omterm{prop:g8:lenses-images:realimage}{real
image} near its focus; stage two magnifies that \omterm{prop:g5:mirrors-reflection:image}{image}. Faint
starlight is precious --- the front \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens}'s \emph{width}
decides how much of it is harvested per \omterm{def:g4:solar-system:star}{star}: aperture is
everything. Microscope, stage one: a strongly curved,
short-$f$ \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens} hung close over the droplet throws a greatly
enlarged \omterm{prop:g8:lenses-images:realimage}{real image}; stage two magnifies again. Its object is
bright and near --- what stage one needs is not width but
bending power. Same architecture, opposite appetites.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g8:lenses-images:1}
\textbf{1.} Touch: bulging middle, converging; hollow middle,
diverging. Window test: \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{converging lenses} throw an upside-down
window on the wall at some distance; \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{diverging lenses} never
do.
\textbf{2.} $f = \qty{25}{cm}$; converging.
\textbf{3.} Diverging --- no \omterm{prop:g8:lenses-images:realimage}{real image} at any distance, and
the shrunken view through it seals the verdict.
\textbf{4.} C: the shorter \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:focus}{focal length} (\qty{10}{cm}) marks
the fatter, harder-bending \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens}.
\textbf{5.} Distant blur with easy near vision: short sight
--- the eye bends too strongly. Prescribe the diverging
family.
\textbf{6.} Arm's-length reading: the aging, weakly-focusing
long-sighted eye. Prescribe converging reading lenses.
\textbf{7.} Reading lenses add bending strength budgeted for
near pages; aimed at mountains, whose rays arrive parallel
and need no help, the added strength over-bends --- \omterm{prop:g5:mirrors-reflection:image}{images}
assemble before the retina, and the peaks blur.
\textbf{8.} Adding correction to a well-focused eye unbalances
it: the trial \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens} bends \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} the child's eye then must
fight, assembling \omterm{prop:g5:mirrors-reflection:image}{images} off the retina --- swimming charts
are the healthy eye's protest.
\textbf{9.} The front \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens} gathers the \omterm{def:g4:solar-system:star}{star}'s parallel rays
into a small \omterm{prop:g8:lenses-images:realimage}{real image} near its focus; the \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{eye-lens} is a
magnifier hung inside its own \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:focus}{focal length} of that \omterm{prop:g5:mirrors-reflection:image}{image},
enlarging it into the observer's eye.
\textbf{10.} Each \omterm{def:g4:solar-system:star}{star} delivers only a whisper of \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} per
square \omterm{def:g2:measuring-length:units}{centimetre}; the saucer-wide \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{lens} collects \omterm{def:g2:measuring-length:units}{centimetres}
by the hundred, funneling a whole dinner-plate of whisper
into one bright point. Width is the astronomer's appetite.
\textbf{11.} The coin-small magnifier, however strongly
curved, would harvest a coin's worth of starlight where a
saucer's worth is needed: \omterm{prop:g5:mirrors-reflection:image}{images} sharp perhaps, but starved
--- faint \omterm{def:g4:solar-system:star}{stars} gone. Gained: nothing the \omterm{def:g8:lenses-images:families}{eye-lens} did not
already give. Verdict: no replacement; order proper glass.
\textbf{12.} For example: ``Everything on this shelf does two
verbs: \emph{gather} \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light}, then \emph{deliver} it where the
eye needs it --- and civilization's whole optical toolkit,
from spectacles to observatories, is those two verbs in
different sizes.''
\end{solution}
