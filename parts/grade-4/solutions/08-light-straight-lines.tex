\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:g4:light-straight-lines} --- Light Travels in Straight Lines}

\begin{solution}{exo:g4:light-straight-lines:1}
\omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{Light} travels in straight lines --- it never curves around
corners. Sightings: straight sunbeams through mist or blinds, the
sharp blade of a torch in dusty \omterm{def:g2:air-around-us:air}{air}, a laser's dead-straight
thread, lighthouse beams.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g4:light-straight-lines:2}
A straight arrowed line drawn to stand for one thread of \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light}: it
shows the road the \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} takes (ruler-straight) and the direction
it runs.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g4:light-straight-lines:3}
Exactly when flame, all three holes and your eye stand on one
straight line. Sliding the middle card a finger's width kills the
view: \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} will not jog sideways --- no straight line, no \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g4:light-straight-lines:4}
Rays run straight from the lamp; the blocker stops some, and the
rays just missing its edge run straight on. The boundary between
reached and unreached ground is drawn by straight lines --- hence
the crisp edge.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g4:light-straight-lines:5}
\omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{Light} leaves the lamp, runs straight to the page, bounces off the
paper, and runs straight from the page into your eye --- which
receives it. Two straight legs with one bounce between.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g4:light-straight-lines:6}
\omterm{def:g3:sound-around-us:sound}{Sound} spreads like pond rings and washes around the corner's edge
to your ear. \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{Light} keeps to straight roads, and no straight line
joins the friend's face to your eye through brick --- so the voice
arrives and the sight does not.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g4:light-straight-lines:7}
Only from the spots where a straight line through the keyhole
reaches the lamp --- eye, keyhole and lamp aligned. Step aside and
the line breaks on the door.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g4:light-straight-lines:8}
The flash: \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light}'s trip counts as instant, while the bang jogs over
at \qty{340}{m} per second --- three silent seconds per kilometre
of distance.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g4:light-straight-lines:9}
Nothing leaves the eye. The \omterm{def:g4:solar-system:star}{star}'s own \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} travels the straight
road \emph{to} you, and seeing is catching it --- the eye is a net,
not a lantern.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g4:light-straight-lines:10}
Stand the shiny surface at the doorway, tilted at a slant (half a
right angle) to the corridor. A ray running along the corridor hits
the tilted mirror and its road folds: it turns the corner and runs
straight through the doorway into the room to your eye. Two such
folds, one above the other, make the submarine's periscope.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g4:light-straight-lines:11}
You are not seeing the \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} in flight --- you are seeing the
countless tiny droplets and dust specks that the beam strikes on
its way, each one bouncing a little \omterm{def:g1:light-and-shadows:source}{light} to your eye. In perfectly
clean \omterm{def:g2:air-around-us:air}{air} there is nothing to strike, and the beam is invisible
from the side: only the bright spot where it lands betrays it.
\end{solution}
