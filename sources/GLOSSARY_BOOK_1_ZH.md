(`sources/TRANSLATION_BOOKS_1-5_ZH.md`). One line per group of English surface
forms: `chapter | English | Chinese`. Chapter `00` means "every chapter whose
English text uses the term" (Book 1 has nine years, so chapter numbers repeat).
Use the Chinese term exactly. If a term reads wrong in your sentence, keep it and
rephrase around it: a synonym is never linked, and the reader meets two words
for one notion. A note in brackets gives the sense; never print the note.
